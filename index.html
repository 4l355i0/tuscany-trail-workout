Purpose

The goal is to create an approximately 45-minute indoor workout that preserves the meaningful power demands of each Tuscany Trail day: climbs, surges, peak efforts and their order. Long descents and very low-power sections are deliberately compressed.

1. GPX to gradient

Elevation is resampled every 100 m and smoothed. Gradient is calculated over approximately 500 m.

Gradient [%] = 100 × Δh / Δs

FTP does not define route power. It is only used to interpret difficulty.

4. Smoothing and macro-blocks

Neighbouring 500 m power estimates are smoothed and similar consecutive segments are merged.

5. Route scaling

Pscaled = Scale factor × P100%

75% means 75% of estimated route power, not 75% of FTP.

6. Time compression

TSS = hours × IF² × 100
IF = NP / FTP

Easy sections are compressed more strongly than meaningful climbs and surges.

7. Low-power sections

Very easy route sections are consolidated and represented around 85 W in the reference workout because literal coasting has little indoor training value.

8. Smart-trainer rationalisation

Micro-intervals are removed; final blocks are generally at least about one minute.

10. Limitations

This is a training model, not a route simulator. It does not reproduce the nutritional, musculoskeletal or positional demands of 4–5 outdoor hours.
